# Task 1 notes

## Audience
Proposal Managers / Proposal Operations Leads handling government RFP responses and compliance packages.

## Decision
Chosen because Task 3 research identified it as the clearest first wedge: explicit owner, repeated document work, public inputs, and a demonstrable source-to-response workflow.

## Channel
Outbound preparation. The machine creates a ranked research queue and personalized draft, but does not contact anyone.

## Cut
No live enrichment API, email sender, CRM, or paid data provider. The goal is a free, deterministic machine that can be inspected and run twice with fresh inputs.
